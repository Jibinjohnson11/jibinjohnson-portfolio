Questions about SEO services.

How long does SEO take to work?

Most campaigns show movement in three to six months. Competitive keywords take longer, and a technically broken site can improve sooner once it's fixed.

Can you guarantee position one on Google?

No, and nobody honestly can. I can show the positions I've achieved for current clients and send you a monthly report on rankings, clicks and work done.

How much do SEO services cost?

Monthly SEO starts from ₹8,000. The quote depends on the number of keywords, the competition and how much content is needed.

Do I need a new website for SEO?

Usually not. I start with an audit of your current site and fix what's there. I recommend a rebuild only when the existing site can't be made fast or crawlable.
