Questions about AI development.

What can AI do for a small business website?

The most useful starting points are an assistant that answers common questions from your own content, and automation of repetitive tasks such as drafting replies or summarising enquiries.

Will an AI chatbot give wrong answers?

It can, so I limit it to your own content, test it against real questions and add a handover to a person when it isn't confident.

Is my business data safe?

I agree with you in writing which data the system may use and where it's processed, and I keep credentials and customer records out of prompts and code.

How much does AI development cost?

It depends on the scope. A website assistant is quoted as an add-on to a site, and AI features in a web application are included in that project's fixed quote.
