I'm Jibin Johnson, a web developer and SEO specialist based in Trivandrum. I design and build websites, then do the work that gets them found: technical SEO, on-page structure and content planning.

I completed a BCA at the University of Madras and a postgraduate diploma in artificial intelligence at Edure, Trivandrum, followed by generative AI training at Blackbox Learning. Today I lead SEO for three businesses and build websites for startups.

I'm now going deeper into full-stack development with Python and Django, building back ends and applications. I use AI tools to research, prototype and test faster, and I review every result myself.
