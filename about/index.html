02Story

From data and AI to websites that rank.

I came into web development through AI. My postgraduate training in artificial intelligence taught me Python, data analysis, machine learning and NLP: how to understand a problem, test an idea and measure the result.

When I moved into building websites, I kept that habit. A site isn't finished when it looks good. It's finished when people can find it, use it and get in touch. That's why I learned SEO alongside development, and why I plan the search structure before I write any code.

Today I build websites, run SEO campaigns, lead a team of interns and keep growing as a full-stack developer, with a long-term focus on AI and automation.

04How I work

Principles I build by.

  1. Keywords and page structure come first, so the site launches ready to compete.

  2. Speed and clarity matter more to visitors and to Google than effects.

  3. AI tools help me research and build faster; every decision and every line is reviewed by me.

  4. Rankings, clicks and enquiries, reported honestly.

  5. Every project teaches something the next one uses.
