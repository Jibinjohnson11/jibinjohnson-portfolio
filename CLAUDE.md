# jibinjohnson.in

Personal website of a web developer and SEO specialist in Trivandrum (Thiruvananthapuram), Kerala.

## Stack
- Plain HTML, CSS, JavaScript. No frameworks, no build step.
- Hosting: Cloudflare Pages (publishes the repo root, no build step; every push to `main` deploys live). Redirects in `_redirects` (301s), headers in `_headers` (immutable `/assets/*` caching, security headers); `404.html` at the root is served for missing pages. The www → apex redirect is a Cloudflare dashboard rule (Pages `_redirects` can't redirect by domain). Contact form: Web3Forms (`action` https://api.web3forms.com/submit, hidden `access_key`, `subject`, `from_name` and a hidden `botcheck` honeypot; `main.js` sends it with fetch, no reload).
- Styles: `assets/css/main.css`. Scripts: `assets/js/main.js`. Images: `assets/img/`.
- Each page is a folder with `index.html`. Pages use relative paths (`../assets/...`); `404.html` uses root-absolute paths (`/assets/...`).

## Pages
`/` (home), `about/`, `services/`, `projects/`, `contact/`, `privacy/`, `404.html`, and six service pages, in this order everywhere (home Services, Services page, footer, OfferCatalog, sitemap, llms.txt, "Other services" lists):
01 Website design and development `website-development-kerala/`, 02 SEO `seo-services-kerala/`, 03 Shopify store development `shopify-developer-kerala/`, 04 E-commerce website development `ecommerce-website-development-kerala/`, 05 Full-stack and software development `full-stack-developer-kerala/`, 06 AI development `ai-developer-kerala/`.
Python and Django is no longer a service (its page was deleted; `_redirects` 301-redirects `/python-django-developer-kerala/*` to `/full-stack-developer-kerala/`). Python and Django stay as tools (toolkit, full-stack and AI pages). There are no Shopify or e-commerce projects yet: never invent case studies, client names, results or certifications for them, and add nothing to Projects for them. Service pages: hero, `.incl` list (6; the SEO page has a 7th, "AEO and GEO", as `li.incl-new`: full row at two and three columns, `--tint` background, a small "New" label `.incl-tag`), a `.split` section, `.steps` process (new pages), a Trivandrum `.split` H2 section, FAQ with FAQPage schema, "Other services" (the other five).


## Rules
- Never put the name "Jibin Johnson" in an H1 or H2. The logo is the word "Jibin".
- Exactly one H1 per page. Keep each page's primary keyword in its title, H1 and meta description.
- Design: warm and light (Apple/Linear-style marketing pages) with a sage green accent and glass details. The page background is pure white; the site has two dark areas only: one dark band on the home page (the "why" section, `.sec.dark`) and the contact panel + footer on every page (`.end`).
- Colours come only from the CSS variables at the top of `main.css`: `--bg` #FFFFFF (page), `--surface` #FFFFFF (alternate bands and cards), `--tint` #EAF0EA (tints, selection), `--ink` #0B0E14, `--muted` #5B606B, `--line` rgba(11,14,20,.10), `--accent` #56685A (deep sage, accent text), `--accent-btn` #637665 (sage, buttons and links), `--accent-btn-hover` #4F6051, `--accent-on-dark` #93AE96, `--glow` #A9C4AB, `--grad` (the accent text gradient #637665 → #56685A → #47574A) and `--glass-dark` rgba(36,46,38,.62). The service icon cards use their own `--card-*` variables; use them only there. Transparent rgba versions of palette colours are allowed. The burgundy accent (#7B1E35, #5E1628, #D96380, #E07A93, #9A2B45, #561326, #F4E6EA), the Petrol Slate Blue accent (#2F526B, #233F52, #598FB6, #E6EEF3), the orange accent (#E8590C, #C2410C, #A3360A, #FF7A1A, the peach #F7E3D5) and the older rust red and orange tints (#A43726, #862C1E, #D56752, #F8E9E5, #EA580C, #FFF1E8) must not come back anywhere, including SVGs, the favicon and og.png. theme-color is #FFFFFF.
- Contrast: body text must reach 4.5:1 and large text (24px+) 3:1. `--accent` #56685A passes for text of any size (5.96:1 on white, 5.15:1 on `--tint`); `--accent-btn` #637665 is 4.87:1 on white and white on it 4.87:1, on the hover 6.72:1. The accent gradient's lightest stop is 4.87:1 on white (4.21:1 on `--tint`, large text only there). Secondary text uses `--muted`. On dark (`.dark`, `.end`: `--ink` background, white text): `--accent` is redefined as `--accent-on-dark` #93AE96 (8.1:1 on `--ink`, 6.49:1 on the dark glass cards) because the sage accents are under 3.7:1 there; links and small text are white, muted text is white at 72%, borders are white at 8–16%, and the focus outline is white.
- Fonts: two families only, Instrument Serif (`--display`) and Switzer (`--font`); no third family (Montserrat, once used in the home Services section, was removed; its file and the unused `Switzer-VariableItalic.woff2` are in `~/Documents/website-backups/original-fonts/`). Switzer licence (ITF Free Font License): never subset, rename internally or modify the ITF font files in any way, and never load them from a CDN. Switzer has no italic face: don't set Switzer text in italic (`.scene em,.scene i` are upright). `font-display: swap`.
