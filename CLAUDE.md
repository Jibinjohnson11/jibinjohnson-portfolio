# jibinjohnson.in

Personal website of a web developer and SEO specialist in Trivandrum (Thiruvananthapuram), Kerala.

## Stack
- Plain HTML, CSS, JavaScript. No frameworks, no build step.
- Hosting: Netlify (`netlify.toml` publishes the repo root; contact form uses Netlify Forms).
- Styles: `assets/css/main.css`. Scripts: `assets/js/main.js`. Images: `assets/img/`.
- Each page is a folder with `index.html`. Pages use relative paths (`../assets/...`); `404.html` uses root-absolute paths (`/assets/...`).

## Pages
`/` (home), `about/`, `services/`, `projects/`, `contact/`, `privacy/`, `404.html`, and six service pages, in this order everywhere (home Services, Services page, footer, OfferCatalog, sitemap, llms.txt, "Other services" lists):
01 Website design and development `website-development-kerala/`, 02 SEO `seo-services-kerala/`, 03 Shopify store development `shopify-developer-kerala/`, 04 E-commerce website development `ecommerce-website-development-kerala/`, 05 Full-stack and software development `full-stack-developer-kerala/`, 06 AI development `ai-developer-kerala/`.
Python and Django is no longer a service (its page was deleted; `netlify.toml` 301-redirects `/python-django-developer-kerala/*` to `/full-stack-developer-kerala/`). Python and Django stay as tools (toolkit, full-stack and AI pages). There are no Shopify or e-commerce projects yet: never invent case studies, client names, results or certifications for them, and add nothing to Projects for them. Service pages: hero, `.incl` list (6; the SEO page has a 7th, "AEO and GEO", as `li.incl-new`: full row at two and three columns, `--tint` background, a small "New" label `.incl-tag`), a `.split` section, `.steps` process (new pages), a Trivandrum `.split` H2 section, FAQ with FAQPage schema, "Other services" (the other five).


## Rules
- Never put the name "Jibin Johnson" in an H1 or H2. The logo is the word "Jibin".
- Exactly one H1 per page. Keep each page's primary keyword in its title, H1 and meta description.
- Design: warm and light (Apple/Linear-style marketing pages) with a sage green accent and glass details. The page background is pure white; the site has two dark areas only: one dark band on the home page (the "why" section, `.sec.dark`) and the contact panel + footer on every page (`.end`).
- Colours come only from the CSS variables at the top of `main.css`: `--bg` #FFFFFF (page), `--surface` #FFFFFF (alternate bands and cards), `--tint` #EAF0EA (tints, selection, featured pricing card), `--ink` #0B0E14, `--muted` #5B606B, `--line` rgba(11,14,20,.10), `--accent` #56685A (deep sage, accent text), `--accent-btn` #637665 (sage, buttons and links), `--accent-btn-hover` #4F6051, `--accent-on-dark` #93AE96, `--glow` #A9C4AB, `--grad` (the accent text gradient #637665 → #56685A → #47574A) and `--glass-dark` rgba(36,46,38,.62). The service icon cards use their own `--card-*` variables; use them only there. Transparent rgba versions of palette colours are allowed. The burgundy accent (#7B1E35, #5E1628, #D96380, #E07A93, #9A2B45, #561326, #F4E6EA), the Petrol Slate Blue accent (#2F526B, #233F52, #598FB6, #E6EEF3), the orange accent (#E8590C, #C2410C, #A3360A, #FF7A1A, the peach #F7E3D5) and the older rust red and orange tints (#A43726, #862C1E, #D56752, #F8E9E5, #EA580C, #FFF1E8) must not come back anywhere, including SVGs, the favicon and og.png. theme-color is #FFFFFF.
- Contrast: body text must reach 4.5:1 and large text (24px+) 3:1. `--accent` #56685A passes for text of any size (5.96:1 on white, 5.15:1 on `--tint`); `--accent-btn` #637665 is 4.87:1 on white and white on it 4.87:1, on the hover 6.72:1. The accent gradient's lightest stop is 4.87:1 on white (4.21:1 on `--tint`, large text only there). Secondary text uses `--muted`. On dark (`.dark`, `.end`: `--ink` background, white text): `--accent` is redefined as `--accent-on-dark` #93AE96 (8.1:1 on `--ink`, 6.49:1 on the dark glass cards) because the sage accents are under 3.7:1 there; links and small text are white, muted text is white at 72%, borders are white at 8–16%, and the focus outline is white.
- Fonts: Instrument Serif (H1, H2, the statement line `.shout`, and large numbers: `.amt`, process step numbers) and Switzer (everything else: body, nav, buttons, forms, labels, H3s, footer; except the home Services section, which uses Montserrat), via `--display` and `--font` in `main.css`. Files in `assets/fonts/`: `Switzer-Variable.woff2` (wght 100–900), `Switzer-VariableItalic.woff2` (declared, not used yet), `InstrumentSerif-Regular.woff2` (400) and `InstrumentSerif-Italic.woff2` (italic 400, the accent line). Instrument Serif is OFL, converted from the TTFs (originals in `~/Documents/website-backups/original-fonts/`); it has one weight only, so every `--display` rule uses `font-weight: 400` (never 500/700, which would fake bold). Switzer licence (ITF Free Font License): never subset, rename internally or modify the ITF font files in any way, and never load them from a CDN. `font-display: swap`. Every page preloads `Switzer-Variable.woff2` and `InstrumentSerif-Regular.woff2` only (`as="font"`, `type="font/woff2"`, `crossorigin`); the italic is not preloaded. Neither Switzer nor Instrument Serif has ₹ (U+20B9), so the `"Rupee Fallback"` face (in both `--font` and `--display`) supplies only ₹ via `unicode-range` from `assets/fonts/plus-jakarta-sans.woff2` (our own subset of the OFL Plus Jakarta Sans; it may be re-subset). Plus Jakarta Sans is used for nothing else. Metric-matched local fallbacks (`"Switzer Fallback"` on Arial, `"Instrument Serif Fallback"` on Times New Roman, with size-adjust and ascent/descent/line-gap overrides) prevent layout shift; recompute them if the font files change. No Google Fonts links.
- Type: H1, H2 and the statement line in Instrument Serif (400) with near-zero tracking (H1 -0.01em, H2 -0.005em, home H1 and statement -0.015em); body Switzer 400 at 17px, line-height 1.65. The accent line of each H1 (`.ln.it`, e.g. "websites that rank.") is Instrument Serif Italic (400) with the accent gradient. The home H1 (line-height 1, `clamp(3rem, 5.1vw, 4.875rem)`) is the largest title at every width, matched only by the home statement line (`.shout`, a `<p>`, not a heading; its last word is `.hl` with the accent gradient); inner-page H1s (`.hero.sub h1`, `clamp(2.25rem, .75rem + 3.45vw, 4rem)`) and the privacy/404 H1 (`.plain h1`) are smaller; H2s (`clamp(2rem, 1.3rem + 2.15vw, 3.25rem)`) smaller again. Inner-page H1s below 600px flow inline and balance at `clamp(1.875rem, calc(10.5vw - .25rem), 2.25rem)`, so `Website&nbsp;development` fits at 360px.

- Phone headings: no single word alone on a line in any H1 from 360 to 390px, and no overflow or sideways scroll.
