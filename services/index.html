FAQ

Questions before you start.

How much does a website cost in Kerala?

It depends on the number of pages, the features you need and whether you need SEO, e-commerce or custom functionality. After a free call about your business, you get a fixed quote in writing before any work begins, so there are no surprises. Domain and hosting are billed separately.

How long does it take to build a website?

A landing page takes about a week and a business website two to three weeks once the content is ready. Web applications are scoped individually. Missing text and photos cause most delays, so I send a content checklist at the start.

Is SEO included with the website?

Every website I build is SEO-ready at launch: keyword-mapped pages, clean headings, meta tags, schema markup, fast loading and Search Console setup. Ongoing SEO, which covers content, links and monthly reporting, is a separate monthly service.

Will I own my website?

Yes. The domain, hosting, source code and all logins are registered in your name, and I hand everything over at launch.

What happens after the website goes live?

You get 30 days of free fixes for anything that doesn't work as agreed. After that, I can quote for maintenance or new features whenever you need them.

Do you work with clients outside Trivandrum?

Yes. I'm based in Trivandrum (Thiruvananthapuram) and meet local clients in person. Clients elsewhere in Kerala, India and abroad work with me over calls and WhatsApp.
