Questions about website development.

How much do website development services cost in Kerala?

My business websites start from ₹12,000 and landing pages from ₹6,000. You get a fixed quote in writing once we've agreed the pages and features. Domain and hosting are billed separately.

How long does a business website take?

Two to three weeks for a typical business website once the content is ready, and about a week for a landing page.

Can you redesign my existing website?

Yes. I keep the existing URLs where possible and set up 301 redirects where they change, so the rankings you already have carry over to the new site.

Can I update the website myself?

Small text and image changes are covered during the 30 days of free fixes. If you need to edit content regularly, I build the site on Django with an admin panel so you can do it without touching code.
