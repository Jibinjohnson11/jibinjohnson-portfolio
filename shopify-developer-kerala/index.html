Why your store needs search built in.

Most Shopify stores launch with the same search problems: titles repeated from the theme, slow pages weighed down by apps, and thin product pages with a single line of description. Google has little reason to rank them, so the store relies on ads from day one.

I'm a web developer and an SEO specialist, so I build the store and the search structure together. Collections follow what your customers search for, every product page gets its own title and description, and the theme stays fast. You launch with a store Google can understand.

Questions about Shopify stores.

How much does a Shopify store cost?

The quote depends on the number of products, the theme work and the apps you need, and you get it in writing before work begins. Shopify bills its monthly plan and any paid apps or themes to you directly.

How long does it take to set up a Shopify store?

Usually two to three weeks once your product details and photos are ready. A large catalogue or heavy theme changes take longer, and the timeline is agreed in the written scope.

Can you move my existing store to Shopify?

Yes. I move your products and collections, and customers and past orders where the old platform allows it. I set up 301 redirects from the old URLs, so the rankings you already have carry over.

Can I manage products myself after launch?

Yes. Shopify's admin is built for that: you add products, change prices and handle orders without code. I walk you through it at handover, and the 30 days of free fixes cover anything that doesn't work as agreed.
