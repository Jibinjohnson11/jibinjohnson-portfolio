Shopify or a custom store?

Shopify suits most shops that want to launch fast and manage everything themselves: products, orders and payments all run from Shopify's admin. If that sounds like your business, Shopify store development is the quicker, lower-cost route.

A custom store suits businesses with special pricing, workflows or integrations: different prices for wholesale and retail customers, made-to-order products, or orders that need to reach your own billing or stock system. You own the code and aren't tied to a platform's plans or apps.

I'll tell you which one fits after the first call.

Questions about e-commerce websites.

How much does an e-commerce website cost in Kerala?

I write a scope listing every page and feature, and quote a fixed price against it before work begins. Domain, hosting and payment gateway fees are billed separately, in your name.

How long does an e-commerce website take?

Usually five to eight weeks, depending on the number of products, the payment and delivery rules and any integrations. The timeline is agreed in the written scope.

Which payment methods can customers in India use?

I integrate Razorpay, which accepts UPI, credit and debit cards, net banking and wallets. Payments go to your own Razorpay account, and cash on delivery can be added as an option.

Can I manage the store myself?

Yes. You get an admin panel to add products, change prices and stock, and handle orders without touching code. I walk you through it at handover, and the 30 days of free fixes cover anything that doesn't work as agreed.
